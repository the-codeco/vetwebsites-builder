Backup

A backup is one .zip file holding your pages, posts, categories, menus, appearance and SEO settings, and every uploaded image. Keep copies somewhere other than this server.

Not included, on purpose: user accounts (their password hashes would travel with the file), the site's domain, email settings, analytics and sent newsletters. For a copy of the whole database, see the README.
