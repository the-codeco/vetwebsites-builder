Search & sharing

Defaults for the whole site. Each page and post can override them in its own Search & sharing section.

Use this while the site is being built. It adds a “noindex” tag to every page, turns off the sitemap and tells crawlers to stay away. It is a request, not a lock: well-behaved search engines respect it, but it doesn't keep the site private.
