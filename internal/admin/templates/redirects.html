New redirect

Moving from an old site, such as Joomla?
  • Addresses with a ? part, such as /index.php?option=com_content&view=article&id=5&Itemid=105: paste the old address as it is. It matches when a visitor's address has the same values; values you leave out, such as Itemid, can be anything.
  • A whole group of addresses: end the old address with /* and put a * in the new one. /index.php/* going to /* sends /index.php/about-us and /index.php/about-us.html to /about-us, and /index.php itself to the home page.
  • A redirect for one address always wins over a /* group, so you can send the few pages that changed name to their new place.
