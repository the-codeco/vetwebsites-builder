Logo and tab icon

Show your logo in the header instead of (or beside) the site title, and set the small picture on browser tabs. Choose pictures from the media library; a PNG or SVG with a clear background works best.

Your own fonts

Upload a font as a WOFF2 file (up to 600 KB) and it appears in the font lists under “Colours, fonts and spacing”. Fonts are served from your own site, so visitors' browsers make no requests to anyone else. Upload a Regular file and, if you like, a Bold file with the same name. Only upload fonts you have the licence to use on a website.

Cookie notice

A banner at the bottom of every page. This site sets no tracking cookies of its own, so you only need it if your visitors expect one or your local rules require it.

“Choice” records the answer in the visitor's own browser and makes it available to the site's features; nothing is sent to the server. Today nothing on the site depends on it. If you change the notice, visitors are asked again.

Footer

Content shown at the bottom of every page, above the bottom line: opening hours, an address, a few links, a short about. Fill in up to four columns; empty ones are left out. Text can be plain (a blank line starts a new paragraph) or basic HTML such as <a href="/contact">. Anything unsafe is removed on save. Links for the footer menu are set under Menus.
